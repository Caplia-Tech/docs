Dashboards are configurable widget grids over your deal flow — pipeline funnels, stage counts, activity feeds, thesis-fit distributions, readiness gaps, needs-attention queues, and the **Pattern Intelligence** family (decision-pattern analysis with recommendations).

## Working with dashboards

- Every team has a **default dashboard** — it's your landing page on login and when switching teams. Admins choose which one.
- Create additional dashboards for specific jobs (a cohort dashboard filtered to `Cohort = Q3`, a partner-meeting dashboard…).
- Widgets take the same filters as deal lists — stage, property, office, thesis — so a dashboard can be scoped to any slice of the pipeline.

## Organisations & offices

Dashboards respect the office lens: an office sees org-owned dashboards computed over its own deals. Admins can publish org-wide dashboards so every office gets the same layout.
