## Pipelines & stages

A **pipeline** is a set of stages a deal moves through (e.g. New → Screening → Meeting → Diligence → Decision, plus terminal Won/Passed stages). Teams can run several pipelines — a deal pipeline and a programme/cohort pipeline, say — with stages renamed, reordered, and activated per pipeline. New deals enter at the pipeline's **first active stage**.

Marking a deal **Won** or **Passed** prompts for structured reasons (multi-select) and terms, which are saved as deal notes for later review.

## Lists & views

Deal lists are where day-to-day screening happens:

- **Layouts** — table, kanban (by stage), grouped, or cards.
- **Filters** — stage, owner, source, sector, time window, office, thesis, and any custom property. A "None" option catches deals where a property isn't set.
- **Columns** — add from a large registry (score, CRI, round data, metrics, dates, **Directory** membership…). The column picker scrolls — everything under the *Company*, *Round*, *Metrics* and *Outcome* headings is available too.
- **Saved views** — save any filter/column/layout combination as a named list, personal or shared with the team. Built-in lists (Deal Flow, Screening, Portfolio…) are read-only; "Save as new list" to customise.

<Tip>
  Selecting rows opens the **bulk action bar** — assign an owner or office, re-score against theses, set a property, add to a directory, or remove from the pipeline in one gesture. See [Properties & bulk actions](/venture-portal/properties-bulk-actions).
</Tip>
