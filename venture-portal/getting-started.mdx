## Teams, organisations, and offices

Everything in the Venture Portal is scoped to a **team**:

- **Solo investors** work in a personal team.
- **Firms** share one team — deals, views, theses, and dashboards are shared.
- **Organisations with offices** (e.g. a multi-site accelerator) get a two-level structure: the organisation owns the pipelines, theses, and properties; each **office** sees its own lens of the shared deal flow. Switch context with the team switcher (bottom of the sidebar).

<Note>
  When you log in — or switch teams — you land on that team's **default dashboard**. Admins can set which dashboard is the default.
</Note>

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Full day-to-day use: deals, notes, scoring, chat |
| **Admin** | Everything above plus team settings, theses, invitations, and directory management |

## First steps for a new team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    **Team → Invite** — teammates join with shared access to the same pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a thesis">
    Head to **Thesis** and encode your mandate — every deal that arrives is scored against it automatically. See [Thesis Builder](/venture-portal/thesis).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an intake channel">
    Add companies manually, by deck upload, or set up an always-on channel (email, form, API). See [Adding companies](/venture-portal/adding-companies).
  </Step>
</Steps>
