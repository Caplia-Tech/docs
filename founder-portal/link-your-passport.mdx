Accelerators and programmes on Caplia (like SETsquared) issue **programme codes**. Redeeming one links your Passport to their programme — they see your company in their pipeline, and you typically receive a funded Caplia licence as part of the programme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Redeem your code">
    Enter the code you were given where prompted (or follow the programme's invite link). One code redemption per company — if you're told a code is already used, your team may have already linked it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The programme reviews your connection">
    High-confidence matches connect automatically; otherwise a programme operator approves it — usually quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're connected">
    On approval: your entitlement (licence) activates, your company appears in the programme's pipeline, and their team scores you against their investment theses automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the programme can see

A linked programme sees your Passport at the level the connection grants — typically your profile, deck, and score. It's the same access model as any investor; you can review it under **Account → Sharing**.

<Note>
  Linking is per-programme. Redeeming SETsquared's code doesn't share anything with other organisations on Caplia.
</Note>
