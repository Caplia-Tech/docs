Your **Caplia Passport** is a single, verified page investors use to evaluate you: company overview, team, traction, deck, key metrics, fundraising round, and your **Caplia Score**.

## The Caplia Score

Your score (0–100) is built from an AI assessment of your materials against a structured rubric — team, market, product, traction, and financial readiness. It updates when you re-upload a deck or materially change your Passport.

- See the full breakdown in **Score Hub**, including what's holding the score back.
- Scores at or above the verification threshold earn a **Verified** badge on your Passport.

<Tip>
  The fastest score improvements usually come from filling *missing* signals (e.g. no financial model uploaded) rather than polishing existing ones.
</Tip>

## Editing your Passport

Use **Investor Preview** to edit your Passport in place — click any field to change it. Two constraints to know:

- **Company description** is limited to 300 characters (with a live counter).
- Deck-derived fields refresh when you upload a new deck version, so edit the deck for anything structural.

## What investors see

Investors view your Passport through the Venture Portal or a share link. They see what you've enabled — profile-only or full data room — and every view is tracked in your engagement analytics.
