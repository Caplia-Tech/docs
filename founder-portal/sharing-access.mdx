You control exactly who sees your Passport and data room, and at what level.

## Ways to share

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Share links" icon="link">
    A URL you can send anywhere. Optional email requirement, password, and expiry. Deactivate any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Access invitations" icon="envelope">
    Invite a specific investor by email at a chosen access level. They land straight on your Passport.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Access requests" icon="hand">
    Investors who find you can request access — you approve at profile-only or full-data-room level.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## NDA-gated access

If you enable NDAs, investors must sign (via Zoho Sign) before full data-room access unlocks. Signed NDAs are stored against the investor's access record.

## Managing access

**Account → Sharing** shows every active grant, pending invitation, and share link:

- **Change access** — move an investor between profile-only and full data room.
- **Revoke** — removes access immediately.
- **Activity** — per-investor view and download history.

<Note>
  Revoking access doesn't delete anything an investor already downloaded — treat downloads as final.
</Note>
