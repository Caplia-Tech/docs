<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.caplia.ai](https://app.caplia.ai) with email, Google, LinkedIn, or Microsoft. If an accelerator gave you a **programme code** or invite link, use that instead — it connects you to their programme automatically (see [Link your Passport](/founder-portal/link-your-passport)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard with Iris">
    Iris walks you through the essentials conversationally: your one-line description, where you're based, and your fundraising timeframe. You can correct anything she gets wrong — just tell her.

    <Note>
      Your company description is capped at **300 characters** — it's the first thing investors read on your Passport, so keep it sharp.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your pitch deck">
    Drop in a PDF or PowerPoint. Caplia extracts your company details, populates your Passport, and kicks off scoring automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your Passport & Score">
    Within a few minutes you'll have a **Caplia Score** and a populated Passport. Head to **Investor Preview** to see exactly what investors will see, and fill any gaps the score highlights.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where things live

| Area | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your score, tasks, and activity at a glance |
| **Score Hub** | The full rubric breakdown behind your Caplia Score |
| **Data Room** | Documents investors can request or view |
| **Investor Preview** | Your Passport exactly as investors see it |
| **Pipeline** | Track the investors you're talking to |
