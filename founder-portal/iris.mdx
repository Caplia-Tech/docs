Iris is Caplia's AI assistant, available throughout the Founder Portal. She knows your company's materials — Passport, deck, data room — and answers in that context.

## What founders use Iris for

- **Onboarding** — she runs your initial setup conversationally and keeps your basics up to date.
- **Improving your materials** — "what's weak in my deck?", "rewrite my one-liner", "what's missing for a seed raise?"
- **Investor prep** — practice answering the questions investors will actually ask based on your score gaps.
- **Quick answers** — anything about your own data ("what did I put as CAC?") or how Caplia works.

## Modes

| Mode | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| **Instant** | Quick factual answers from your materials |
| **Thinking / research** | Deeper questions that need web research or multi-document reasoning |

## Iris on WhatsApp

Link your WhatsApp number under **Account** and Iris can answer on the go — including sending your own documents to you ("send me my pitch deck").
